OUR STORY

Your design, our press.

Casual Wears is a print shop first: send us your artwork, or pick one of ours, and we press it in-house onto soft, everyday blanks — no middlemen, no long waits.

Every order starts fresh from our studio in Kathmandu, made to move with you, whether that's a coffee run, a study session, or doing absolutely nothing at all.
